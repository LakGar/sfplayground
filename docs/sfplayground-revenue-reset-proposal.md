# SFPLAYGROUND Revenue Reset Proposal

Date: September 23, 2026  
Prepared for: SFPLAYGROUND cofounders

## Executive Summary

SFPLAYGROUND needs to move from roughly $10K/month to $50K/month.

The current model is not enough:

- $60 tickets are hard to sell.
- Demo booths are not selling.
- Sponsorships are not closing.
- Industry-specific AI events are losing attendance.

This means the market does not currently value our events as standalone paid products.

We should stop selling "events" and start selling specific outcomes:

- investor access
- customer access
- curated founder/funder rooms
- SF market entry support
- hiring and talent access
- credibility and visibility for founders

## Current Problem

Our offer is too broad.

We are asking people to pay for attendance, booths, or sponsorship before they clearly believe the room will produce value.

Founders do not want booths. They want investors, customers, credibility, and momentum.

Sponsors do not want logo placement. They want pipeline, qualified leads, and strategic relationships.

Attendees do not want another AI mixer. They want access they cannot easily get elsewhere.

## What We Should Stop

Effective immediately, we should stop relying on:

- generic $60 event tickets as the main revenue source
- demo booth sales
- sponsorship as the primary path to revenue
- broad "AI in [industry]" events without committed buyers, investors, or anchor guests
- overlapping events that split our own audience
- events that do not have a clear paid outcome

## New Positioning

SFPLAYGROUND should become:

> The curated access network helping founders break into the San Francisco ecosystem through investor rooms, customer introductions, founder visibility, and high-signal private events.

This is stronger than:

> We host startup events in San Francisco.

## New Revenue Model

The path to $50K/month should come from multiple paid products, not only tickets.

### 1. Founder Access Rooms

Small curated rooms for founders, investors, and operators.

Format:

- 20 accepted founders
- 5-8 investors/operators
- application-first
- accepted founders pay
- structured intros before and after the event
- no booths

Pricing:

- $250-$500 accepted founder seat
- $1,000 premium founder package with pitch review and targeted intros

Target:

- 2 rooms/month
- $7.5K-$12K per room
- $15K-$24K/month

### 2. SF Market Entry Package

For out-of-town, international, or first-time Bay Area founders.

Includes:

- event access
- curated founder/investor/operator intros
- pitch or demo opportunity
- SF ecosystem briefing
- content/photo visibility
- recommended events and rooms to attend

Pricing:

- $2,500 starter
- $5,000 standard
- $7,500 premium

Target:

- 4 clients/month at $5K average
- $20K/month

### 3. Founder Visibility Package

For founders who want credibility, content, and distribution.

Includes:

- founder profile
- event recap placement
- photos/video
- pitch/demo moment
- LinkedIn feature
- newsletter inclusion

Pricing:

- $1,000-$3,000

Target:

- 5 clients/month at $2K average
- $10K/month

### 4. Hiring / Talent Rooms

For AI startups hiring engineers, operators, GTM, or founding team members.

Format:

- startups pay to participate
- candidates attend free or low cost
- curated matching
- no sponsor model required

Pricing:

- $500-$1,500 per hiring company

Target:

- 10 companies at $1K
- $10K/event

## $50K Monthly Target Mix

Recommended monthly plan:

| Revenue source | Monthly target |
|---|---:|
| Founder Access Rooms | $15K |
| SF Market Entry Packages | $20K |
| Founder Visibility Packages | $10K |
| Community tickets / smaller events | $5K |
| Total | $50K |

Sponsorship should be treated as upside, not required revenue.

## 30-Day Plan

### Week 1: Reset the Offer

- Pause weak industry-specific events unless demand is already proven.
- Remove booth-first language from sales.
- Build one core offer: Founder Access Room.
- Build one service offer: SF Market Entry Package.
- Create a simple one-page sales deck for each.

### Week 2: Validate With Warm Leads

Talk to:

- 30 founders
- 10 investors/operators
- 10 international or out-of-town founders

Questions:

- Would you pay $250-$500 for a curated investor/operator access room?
- Would you pay $2,500-$5,000 for SF market entry support?
- What outcome would make this worth it?

Do not build a large event until people say yes.

### Week 3: Run First Paid Test

Launch:

> SFPLAYGROUND Founder Access Room

Goal:

- 20 accepted founders
- 5-8 investors/operators
- $5K-$10K revenue

Rules:

- application-first
- no free mass attendance
- no booths
- structured intros
- post-event follow-up

### Week 4: Package Results

After the first room:

- publish recap
- count intros made
- collect testimonials
- identify repeat buyers
- upsell premium packages
- schedule the next room

## Sales Messaging

Do not say:

> Buy a ticket to our startup event.

Say:

> Apply for a curated founder access room built to connect serious founders with investors, operators, and strategic partners in San Francisco.

Do not say:

> Buy a demo booth.

Say:

> Get structured visibility and targeted introductions inside a curated founder/investor room.

Do not say:

> Sponsor our event.

Say:

> Partner with us only if you want direct access to a curated group of founders that match your target customer or investment thesis.

## Decision Needed

We need alignment on three decisions:

1. Stop treating sponsorships and demo booths as the main growth plan.
2. Shift from broad events to paid access products.
3. Run a 30-day validation sprint before committing to more industry-specific events.

## Recommendation

SFPLAYGROUND should become a paid access network, not just an event company.

The event is the container.

The product is access, trust, curation, and outcomes.
