# Funders & Founders Rooftop Mixer: Applicant and Sponsorship Brief

Analysis date: September 15, 2026  
Event: Thursday, October 8, 2026, 4:00-8:00 PM  
Capacity: 100

## Executive take

The applicant pool is strong enough to support a premium, invite-only sponsorship pitch. The value is not raw volume; it is the concentration of founders, investors, and senior operators in a small room.

The strongest positioning is:

> Attendance is curated. The only guaranteed company access is through an official event partnership.

This is better than saying anyone can buy their way in. Sponsors are buying a thoughtfully hosted presence, a small number of passes, and relevant introductions. Final attendee selection remains with SFPlayground.

## Applicant pool snapshot

The Partiful guest manager showed 217 pending/interested profiles. The rendered table exposed 200 profiles for analysis, so the figures below are directional. Applicant claims have not been independently verified.

- 125 founder/CEO profiles (62.5%)
- 42 investor/funder profiles (21%)
- 13 senior operator/executive profiles (6.5%)
- 19 other or unclear profiles (9.5%)
- 57 profiles with a meaningful traction signal such as revenue, pilots, customers, contracts, users, or notable partnerships
- 31 profiles with an explicit revenue, paid-customer, paid-pilot, or contracted-revenue signal
- 15 profiles tied to recognizable institutional or venture organizations

In practical terms, roughly four out of five applicants are founders or capital allocators. That is the core sponsor story.

## Recommended 100-person room

- 25 investors, angels, family offices, and venture-platform leaders
- 45 founders with funding, revenue, pilots, or unusually strong domain credibility
- 10 senior operators and executives who can become customers, partners, or advisors
- 10 sponsor and ecosystem partner seats
- 10 discretionary seats for speakers, press, community leaders, and unusually promising early founders

Approve quality first. A smaller, well-balanced room is more valuable than filling all 100 seats with undifferentiated founders.

## Sponsorship structure

### Lead Room Partner - $5,000

- 6 guaranteed, named passes, subject to conduct and fit standards
- Top partner placement on the event page and attendee communications
- Opening acknowledgment; no sales pitch from the stage
- Six curated introductions based on mutually agreed target profiles
- Reserved rooftop meeting area for one hour
- Inclusion in the post-event recap and photography
- Aggregate audience summary after the event

### Access Partner - $3,000

- 4 guaranteed, named passes, subject to conduct and fit standards
- Partner placement on the event page
- Opening acknowledgment
- Three curated introductions
- Inclusion in the post-event recap

Target outcome: one Lead Room Partner plus one Access Partner for $8,000 total.

Do not include the attendee email list. Offer opt-in introductions and aggregate audience data only.

## Credibility fixes before outreach

The live Partiful page currently displays both "Public Event" and "100/100 spots left." Those signals conflict with an invite-only, oversubscribed sponsorship pitch. Before sponsor outreach, change the event access setting and public capacity language so the page visibly supports the scarcity claim.

Also avoid presenting pending applicants as confirmed attendees. Use "applicant pool includes" until people are accepted and have confirmed.
