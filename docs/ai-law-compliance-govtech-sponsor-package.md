# AI Law, Compliance & GovTech

## SF Tech Week Partner Brief

**Wednesday, October 7, 2026 | 6:00-9:00 PM**  
**AngelList, 90 Gold St, San Francisco**  
**Capacity: 120 guests**

SFPlayground is convening founders, investors, lawyers, compliance leaders, policy experts, public-sector innovators, and AI builders for an evening focused on the systems that interpret, enforce, and operate rules.

The program will cover legal AI, regulatory automation, AI governance, privacy, contracts, litigation, public-sector technology, procurement, and responsible deployment in regulated industries.

Confirmed speaker: **Owen Zhang, Cofounder and CEO of Global Citizens**
